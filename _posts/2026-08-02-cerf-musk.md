[Vint Cerf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vint_Cerf) considered by many Internet researchers as the father of the Internet has [decided to retire](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/the-father-of-the-internet-is-finally-retiring/) from his Chief Internet Evangelist position at Google at the age of 83.

Ellon Musk claims that future datacenter should be placed in orbit. An [article](https://spectrum.ieee.org/orbital-data-centers-heat) in IEEE Spectrum shows that would be a very difficult challenge from a heat and an energy viewpoint.

Falvio Luciani argues on [RIPE Labs](https://labs.ripe.net/author/flavio_luciani_1/when-internet-traffic-becomes-a-physical-event/) that coping with flash crowds and various events where a large population wants to access the same information will remain a problem for the Internet.

Several web browsers and TLS applications can export the negotiated key material to a SSLKEYLOGFILE which can be used by tools such as [wireshark](https://www.wireshark.org). [RFC9850](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc9850/) provides the following specification of this file format.

An Internet Society [blog post](https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2026/07/measuring-data-center-growth/) provides a graphical representation of the growth of datacenters throughout the world during the last years based on PeeringDB data.


The Switzerland-Columbia math during the FIFA World Cup caused an [all-time traffic peak](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/103-tbps-again-arist-volger-diqde/) at the Swiss Internet Exchange with more than 1Tbsp. 

In July 2024, Bangladeshi disappeared from the Internet for some time. This [blog post](https://labs.ripe.net/author/mdkamruzzaman-khan-2/inside-a-shutdown-bgp-evidence-from-an-operator-who-was-there/) analyses this event from routing data.

Martin Hellman shares in a recent [article](https://cacm.acm.org/author/martin-e-hellman/) in Communications of the ACM the early days of public key cryptography and the creation of the Diffie-Hellman key exchange scheme.
